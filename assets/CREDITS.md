## Audio

The [audio source manifest](audio/audio_sources.csv) lists every shipped audio file with its
source, author and license. Effects include original PairNap synthesis and adapted recordings
from Kenney and Freesound. The recorded card movements are “Card Flip” and “Dealing Card”
by f4ngy (CC BY 4.0). Miron’s movement uses “Cute Kitten Meow” by Breviceps (CC0 1.0). The four owner-supplied soundtrack tracks use the Pixabay Content
License and remain in their original MP3 encoding.
